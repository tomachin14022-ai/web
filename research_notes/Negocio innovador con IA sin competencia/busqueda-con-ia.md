# From classic search to AI answers: how discovery is changing (2025–2026) and which problems are still unserved for Spanish-speaking / LatAm businesses and professionals

Scope note: research done 2026-11-20. Almost all sources are from 2025–2026; older ones are flagged. Many numbers come from vendors that sell AI-visibility or local-listing products (SOCi, Yext, BrightLocal, Searchable, Insites, Similarweb, Local Falcon). They are labeled where that matters. Several primary pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool (openai.com "Introducing Businesses in ChatGPT", Search Engine Land's SOCi write-up, ecosistemastartup.com articles), so those facts come from secondary coverage and search snippets. The OpenAI "Argentina Productivity Report" PDF could not be parsed, so its numbers come from press coverage (BAE Negocios).

## 1. How much discovery has moved to AI answers (hard data, global and LatAm)

### Takeaway
AI answers now sit on top of most discovery, but they add a layer on top of Google rather than replacing it. Google still shows AI Overviews on about 43% of US searches, and about 68% of US Google searches end with no click. ChatGPT has about 0.9–1.2 billion weekly users, and 95% of them still use Google. AI-referred traffic is small in absolute terms but grows several hundred percent a year and converts better than other traffic. Argentina is among the heaviest AI-using countries in the world (68% weekly use, Reuters Institute 2026), and Google's AI Mode has been available in Spanish across Latin America since 23 Sep 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Google is answering instead of linking**
- In the US, Google searches ended without a click 68.01% of the time in Jan–Apr 2026, up from 60.45% in 2024 (SparkToro analysis of Similarweb clickstream data). The data excludes the Google mobile app, where zero-click is probably higher. — [Search Engine Land, 2026 zero-click study](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-reach-68-in-early-2026-study-479717)
- Similarweb's own series: 69% zero-click in May 2025, up from 56% when AI Overviews launched in May 2024. — [SE Roundtable on Similarweb](https://www.seroundtable.com/similarweb-google-zero-click-search-growth-39706.html)
- AI Overviews appeared in about 43% of US searches measured by Similarweb, up from about 15% a year earlier. — [Similarweb AI search stats](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/); [Search Engine Journal on Similarweb](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-isnt-replacing-google-its-layering-on-top-similarweb-data/)
- Pew (July 2025, browsing data from 900 US adults): when an AI summary appeared, users clicked a traditional result on 8% of visits, against 15% without one. They clicked a link inside the summary on 1% of visits. 26% of sessions ended on a page with an AI summary, against 16% without. At the time, 18% of the searches in the study showed an AI summary. Google disputed the methodology. — [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/07/22/google_ai_overviews_suppress_search/)
- AI Mode: Google said at I/O 2026 that AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users and that query volume was more than doubling every quarter. Clickstream data, however, shows only 0.34% of searches moving into AI Mode in Jan–Apr 2026. AI Mode reportedly sends referrals on 1.6–2.5% of queries, against 17–19% for classic Google. — [Search Engine Land zero-click study](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717) (the AI Mode referral-rate figure is secondhand Similarweb data)
- Similarweb says AI Mode "grew fast early, then leveled off and started to soften" (no figures given). — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)

**AI assistants: scale and share**
- ChatGPT reached 900M weekly active users and 50M paying subscribers in Feb 2026, up from 800M in Oct 2025. — [TechCrunch, 27 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users)
- In Nov 2026, coverage of the "Businesses in ChatGPT" launch cites OpenAI as saying 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT weekly and that ChatGPT's search volume is one-fifth of Google's. Outside estimates put the search volume lower. — [Nation's Restaurant News, 12 Nov 2026](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/chatgpt-launches-google-like-profiles-for-businesses) (OpenAI's claim, reported secondhand)
- Reports from Aug–Sep 2026 of "more than 1 billion users" are unclear about whether the figure covers ChatGPT alone or all OpenAI products. — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/openai-has-more-than-1-billion-users-2-million-businesses); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-1-billion-weekly-active-users-openai-target)
- Share of generative-AI web visits, worldwide, June 2025 → May 2026: ChatGPT about 76% → about 53%; Gemini under 9% → about 27–28%; Claude about 2% → about 9%. Meta AI's app MAU growth was "nearly as high as Claude's" (no share given). Gen-AI sites averaged 9.5B monthly visits, up 70% year over year. — [Similarweb Generative AI landscape](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/similarweb-2026-generative-ai-landscape-ai-search-fragmentation)
- 95% of ChatGPT users also used Google, both in Sep 2025 and in May 2026. — [Search Engine Journal on Similarweb](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-isnt-replacing-google-its-layering-on-top-similarweb-data/)
- Share of US ChatGPT prompts whose answer carries a citation (link): about 1.6% (June 2025) → about 6.8% (May 2026). By category: Travel & Hospitality about 23%, Automotive about 20%, Professional Services under 4%. — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)
- Ads in ChatGPT: about 26% of ChatGPT responses contain an ad, and about one-third of ad impressions appear in the first response of a session. — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)
- Around 60% of ChatGPT-referred visits land on a homepage (after a May 2026 change to how ChatGPT links), up from about 26–29% before. — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)

**AI-referred traffic (Adobe)**
- 2025 US holiday season (1 Nov–31 Dec), more than 1 trillion visits to US retail sites: traffic from AI sources rose 693.4% year over year (769% in November, 673% in December). AI referrals converted 31% better than other traffic (54% better on Thanksgiving, 38% on Black Friday) and brought 32% more revenue per visit, a reversal from 2024, when non-AI traffic was worth 51% more. Other industries: travel +539%, banking +344%, tech/software +120%. — [Adobe newsroom, Jan 2026](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/01/adobe-holiday-shopping-season); [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/01/13/generative-ai-online-holiday-shopping-traffic-2025/)
- Caveat: Adobe does not publish the base volume or AI's share of total traffic or revenue, so the growth percentages start from a small base. Salesforce, using a different method, says AI influenced 20% of US Christmas 2025 retail sales. — [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/?p=1559319) (via search summary)

**Argentina and LatAm**
- Reuters Institute "Generative AI and News Report 2026" (published 29 Oct 2026): Argentina has the highest weekly AI use of the nine countries surveyed, at 68%. Weekly use across the six countries tracked since 2024 rose from 34% to 51%. — [Reuters Institute 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/generative-ai-and-news-report-2026-standalone-tools-information-infrastructure); [C5N](https://www.c5n.com/tecnologia/argentina-lidera-el-uso-semanal-inteligencia-artificial-causas-y-evolucion-esta-relacion-estrecha-n253200)
- Reuters Institute 2025 edition (six countries including Argentina; fieldwork June–July 2025, about 2,000 respondents per country): weekly generative-AI use went from 18% to 34%, ChatGPT led at 22% weekly use, and **using AI to find information (24% weekly) overtook using it to create media** as the main use. Weekly use for news was 6%, highest in Argentina and the US. — [Reuters Institute Gen AI and News Report 2025 (PDF)](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2025-10/Gen_AI_and_News_Report_2025.pdf); [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/10/people-are-using-chatgpt-twice-as-much-as-they-were-last-year-theyre-still-just-as-skeptical-of-ai-in-news/)
- OpenAI's Argentina report (Oct 2025): "millions" of Argentines use ChatGPT weekly, and users tripled in a year. Argentina is in LatAm's top five for paid subscriptions and API developers. Usage by province: Buenos Aires 46.6%, **Córdoba 12.3%**, Santa Fe 9.1%, Mendoza 4%, Tucumán 3.2%. Main users are aged 18–34. The top uses are specific tasks (13%), step-by-step recommendations (11%) and tutoring (11%). — [BAE Negocios, 7 Oct 2025](https://www.baenegocios.com/amp/ChatGPT-impulsa-la-productividad-y-el-crecimiento-economico-en-Argentina-segun-OpenAI-20251007-0122/); [OpenAI report PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/global-affairs/0802f1d7-68bc-4325-8b88-30125a6f0aa1/argentina-productivity-report-october-2025.pdf)
- Google launched AI Mode in Spanish worldwide on 23 Sep 2025, including Argentina, Mexico and the rest of Spanish-speaking Latin America (Spain was excluded at launch for EU regulatory reasons). It runs on a custom Gemini 2.5 with query fan-out. — [Google blog (es-419)](https://blog.google/intl/es-419/actualizaciones-de-producto/informacion/buscador-de-google-presentamos-el-modo-ia-en-hispanoamerica/); [Montevideo Portal](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Ciencia-y-Tecnologia/Llego-el-buscador-de-Google-en-espanol-ya-incorpora-modo-IA--uc937721); [iProfesional, AI Mode in Argentina](https://www.iprofesional.com/tecnologia/438142-google-lanza-modo-ia-en-argentina-con-busqueda-inteligente-y-multimodal)
- Meta AI / WhatsApp: WhatsApp passed 3 billion monthly users (Q1 2025 call). Zuckerberg said on the Q2 2026 call (29 Jul 2026) that WhatsApp is "the leading surface where people engage with Meta AI" and that daily Meta AI users rose 60% after the rebuild with Muse Spark. CFO Susan Li said WhatsApp has the strongest Meta AI usage. — [Meta Q2 2026 call transcript](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/META-Q2-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf); [Benzinga transcript](https://www.benzinga.com/node/62044599); [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2025/05/01/whatsapp-crosses-3-billion-monthly-users-as-meta-doubles-down-on-ai-strategy)
- Meta AI was expanded to 22 countries, including Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador and Mexico. — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/tecnologia/innovacion/meta-expande-su-chatbot-de-ia-a-mas-paises-de-america-latina-para-impulsar-su-crecimiento/) (date not confirmed in the snippet, probably 2024)
- "Meta AI handles more than one million weekly conversations in Mexico": headline only, date and method not verified. — [Marketing4eCommerce MX](https://marketing4ecommerce.mx/meta-ai-conversaciones-semanales-whatsapp-mexico/)
- "Meta AI has more than 1 billion monthly users": only an aggregator says so, and I could not match it to a Meta primary disclosure in this session. — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/meta-ai-users/)

### Inferences
- In LatAm the change matters at least as much as in the US. Argentina leads the world in weekly AI use, AI Mode speaks Spanish, and WhatsApp is the main way people use Meta AI. But almost all hard measurement (Adobe, Similarweb, SOCi, BrightLocal, Pew) is US-only. **The lack of LatAm-specific measurement is itself a gap.**
- For a local business, "being the answer" increasingly means being named inside a short AI answer, not ranking in a list of 10 links. The click share keeps falling (8% vs 15%) while AI-referred visitors convert better.
- Córdoba is about 12% of Argentina's ChatGPT usage, which is relevant if he starts locally.

### Gaps
- No Argentina- or LatAm-specific AI-referral-traffic data (Adobe-style) was found.
- No share of searches answered by AI for Argentina or LatAm was found.
- No confirmed Meta AI user count for Brazil, Mexico or Argentina; no data on how often Meta AI in WhatsApp recommends businesses.
- No primary OpenAI figure for ChatGPT weekly users in Argentina (only "millions" and "tripled").

## 2. How AI assistants choose and represent local businesses, products and professionals

### Takeaway
AI assistants recommend far fewer businesses than Google Maps and get many facts about them wrong, and small businesses get more wrong than big ones. The sources they cite are mostly sources the business can control: its own website, Google Business Profile and listings (Yelp in the US). Reddit and forums matter less for local queries than the GEO hype suggests. In November 2026 OpenAI launched "Businesses in ChatGPT", a Google-Business-Profile-like claimable profile. For local stores it seems limited to existing US listings for now.

### Cited Findings
**How often local businesses are recommended**
- SOCi 2026 Local Visibility Index (28 Jan 2026; more than 350,000 locations of 2,751 multi-location brands): ChatGPT recommended **1.2%** of locations, Gemini 11% and Perplexity 7.4%, against 35.9% appearing in Google's local 3-pack. In retail, fewer than half of the brands that did well in classic local search also appeared in AI recommendations. — [NatLawReview press release](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/ai-search-recommends-only-12-local-businesses-rest-are-invisible); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-local-visibility-report-2026-468085); [Localogy](https://www.localogy.com/?p=44138) (vendor study; full report requires registration)
- SOCi also found business-profile information **about 68% accurate on ChatGPT and Perplexity, against 100% on Gemini** (which draws on Google Maps). — same sources
- BrightLocal (16 Sep 2026; about 200,000 non-branded local prompts, about 1,300 locations, 1.97M citations; ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews only): tracked-business mention rate was Google Maps 66%, AI Overviews 38%, ChatGPT 33% and AI Mode 32%. Businesses per answer: ChatGPT 4.1, AI Mode 3.5, AI Overviews 2.5. About 10–11% of answers were empty. Only 20–33% of results overlapped between repeat searches. Storefronts were mentioned 33.6% of the time and service-area businesses 29.4%. ChatGPT's 90% radius is about 287 km, against 39–64 km for Google's surfaces. The study does **not** measure factual accuracy. — [BrightLocal local AI visibility study](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/) (vendor)
- Restaurant example: about 83% of restaurants did not appear at all in ChatGPT, against about 14% on Google. — vendor claim in [PinMeTo](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/chatgpt-storefront-brand-visibility-2026/) (via search summary; not verified)

**What sources they cite**
- BrightLocal citation mix: business websites 42% of citations (93% of unique domains); Google Business Profile 28.63% (present for 94% of businesses); Yelp 9.53%; Facebook 2.23%; Tripadvisor 1.79%; OpenTable 1.18%. **ChatGPT relies little on Google Business Profile.** All three platforms use Yelp, and Google's two use Facebook. — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)
- Yext (9 Oct 2025; 6.8M citations from 1.6M queries per model on ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, Jul–Aug 2025): 86% of citations come from brand-managed sources (first-party websites 44%, listings about 42%); forums such as Reddit only 2%. Listings are 52.6% of citations in healthcare; reviews and social are 13.3% in food service. — [Yext press release / Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251009106549/en/Yext-Research-86-of-AI-Citations-Come-from-Brand-Managed-Sources-Clarifying-How-Marketers-Can-Compete-in-the-AI-Search-Era); [Yext blog](https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed) (vendor that sells listings management; "brand-managed" is Yext's own category)
- Yelp licensed reviews, ratings, photos and business details to OpenAI for ChatGPT answers (reportedly 330M reviews and more than 8M listings). Neither company has published how Yelp content is weighted. — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/yelp-openai-licensing-deal-chatgpt-local-reviews); [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ai/yelp-chatgpt/)
- ChatGPT added local knowledge panels in December 2025. — [SE Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/openai-adds-local-knowledge-panels-to-chatgpt-results-40635.html)
- Fardo, an Argentine AI-visibility consultancy, says (with the agency On The Rocks) that press coverage became the main input for models such as ChatGPT. A related headline says "51% of what AI answers comes from the media". Details not verified. — [Mercado](https://mercado.com.ar/ruta-digital/la-ia-redefine-el-valor-de-la-prensa-los-medios-ganan-peso-como-fuente-de-autoridad-para-las-marcas); [InfoNegocios](https://infonegocios.info/enfoque/la-prensa-planto-bandera-el-51-de-lo-que-responde-la-ia-sale-de-los-medios-y-casi-nada-de-la-pauta)
- Note the conflict: Yext puts forums at 2% of citations, while much GEO marketing stresses Reddit. The difference is probably query type (local and objective queries vs opinion queries), but no source settles it.

**Accuracy: AI misrepresents small businesses**
- Searchable (25 Jun 2026; 165 London businesses, 13,365 responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, checked against LinkedIn and Companies House): **93% of the businesses were described inaccurately**. 50% of SMEs got at least one false fact, against 32% of large companies. Fabrication: 5% for SMEs vs 2% for large companies. Misattribution: 4% vs 0.7%. The weakest fields were company size, website, founding year, phone number and services. — [IT Brief UK](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/london-smes-suffer-ai-misinformation-in-search-tests) (vendor)
- Searchable, Leeds (Jul 2026): the three most-used tools returned at least one false fact for about 57% of the retailers tested. — [Retail Tech Innovation Hub](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/2026/7/15/many-uk-high-street-retailers-are-suffering-from-ai-chatbot-inaccuracies-searchable-research-finds)
- Insites (vendor; 10,000 US local businesses, using Google Business Profile as the reference): only about 56% of ChatGPT answers and 48% of Perplexity answers matched on every detail. Wrong phone numbers were common. — [Insites blog](https://insites.com/blog/does-chatgpt-hallucinate-local-businesses-the-data-on-ai-accuracy/)
- Argentina: iProfesional says ChatGPT, without specific configuration, does not see current interest rates or monthly inflation, and can invent rates or present outdated data with full confidence. — [iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/tecnologia/468562-iadiccion-riesgos-de-pedirle-consejos-economicos-a-chatgpt). Older (2023) test: [Bloomberg Línea, "La inflación argentina dejó sin respuestas a ChatGPT"](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/argentina/la-inflacion-argentina-dejo-sin-respuestas-a-chatgpt/)

**Businesses in ChatGPT (new, Nov 2026)**
- Launched 10 Nov 2026 as a beta. Businesses can claim a profile (website, hours, location, description), add a product list with prices and availability if they sell online, and see a dashboard of mentions, website clicks and conversation types. A "Business Agent" can chat with consumers, but only for eligible e-commerce and online-service businesses in the US. A profile does not guarantee better placement. — [Nation's Restaurant News](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/chatgpt-launches-google-like-profiles-for-businesses); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-lets-you-claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-and-set-up-a-custom-agentic-ai-customer-sales-rep)
- Eligibility "depends on industry, location and verification". No official country list was found. One guide says local stores and restaurants can currently claim only existing US listings (not confirmed by OpenAI). Australia is reported as eligible. Multi-location management is not available yet. — [Rankability](https://www.rankability.com/blog/how-to-claim-your-business-in-chatgpt/); [SmartCompany AU](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/artificial-intelligence/australian-businesses-can-now-claim-themselves-in-chatgpt/); [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/chatgpt-new-business-hub-raises-the-bar-for-location-intelligence-417159a)
- OpenAI is also extending ChatGPT ads to small and local businesses; agency Scorpion already sells ChatGPT ads to local businesses. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/scorpion-brings-chatgpt-ads-local-businesses-platforms-race-ai-discovery-moments); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/openai-expands-chatgpt-advertising-to-small-and-local-businesses-competing-with-google-meta-in-the-space/)

**Professionals and verticals**
- Doctoralia (Docplanner) surveys: 55% have used AI to look up health information; more than 61% value or have used AI chatbots for emotional questions; 9 in 10 patients search for specialists online before booking. Doctoralia says AI "is changing how patients search, compare and choose their doctors". — [El Mundo SV](https://diario.elmundo.sv/salud/el-61-de-los-usuarios-acude-a-chatbots-de-ia-por-dudas-emocionales-alerta-estudio-de-doctoralia); [DPL News](https://dplnews.com/el-medico-que-no-adopte-la-tecnologia-quedara-rezagado-doctoralia/) (company surveys)
- Forbes Chile (1 Jul 2026) calls Doctoralia "the TripAdvisor of doctors", with 3 million visits a month in Chile and a "key hub for AI". — [Forbes Chile](https://forbes.cl/negocios/2026-07-01/una-polaca-y-una-espanola-el-tripadvisor-de-los-medicos-suma-3-millones-de-visitas-al-mes-en-chile-y-hub-clave-para-ia/)
- Small sample: in Nov 2026, Visibility Audit Co tested 20 Florida medical and dental offices; ChatGPT named the office in 20 of 60 answers. — [aisotools listing](https://aisotools.com/tool/visibility-audit-co) (very small sample)
- Car dealers (US): a Savvy Dealer test found 84 of 100 dealer websites scored below 60 ("failing") when ChatGPT was asked for dealer recommendations. Estimates of car shoppers who use AI range from about 30% to 55% (sources conflict). — [Dealership Guy](https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/84-out-of-100-dealership-websites-are-basically-invisible-to-ai-search-2026-01-15); [Dealership Guy: "dealers have no idea how AI is describing them"](https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/dealers-have-no-idea-how-ai-is-describing-them-to-car-buyers-and-that-s-the-problem)

### Inferences
- In LatAm, the US data pipeline for local answers does not exist in the same form: Yelp is barely used in Argentina, and Businesses in ChatGPT local claiming seems US-only. ChatGPT probably falls back to business websites, Facebook, directories, the press and stale web pages for LatAm local answers. SOCi's 68% accuracy figure for ChatGPT is US data; **in Argentina accuracy is likely worse, but this is unmeasured.** It is a testable hypothesis and a possible marketing hook ("measured: X% of Córdoba businesses described wrong").
- Inflation and dual pricing (ARS and USD) make Argentine prices go stale faster than anywhere else, so AI answers about Argentine prices are structurally more likely to be wrong.
- Professional services have the lowest citation presence (under 4%) in ChatGPT answers. When the model does not cite, it answers from memory, which is where fabrication happens. Professionals (doctors, lawyers, psychologists, accountants, architects) are therefore probably the most misrepresented category, but there is no direct measurement.
- Businesses in ChatGPT turns "AI profile management" into a real category, like Google Business Profile management in 2015. When it opens to LatAm local businesses, someone will have to claim, fill in, keep updated and monitor those profiles for SMBs in Spanish.

### Gaps
- No accuracy study of AI answers about LatAm or Spanish-language businesses was found.
- Which sources ChatGPT, Gemini and Meta AI use for local answers in Argentina (Foursquare? Google? Facebook? Mercado Libre? Doctoralia?) is not documented anywhere I found.
- Businesses in ChatGPT: no official country list and no official LatAm timeline.
- No data on how Meta AI inside WhatsApp answers local or business questions.

## 3. Existing GEO / AI-visibility tools: price, coverage, LatAm players, complaints

### Takeaway
The global category is crowded and maturing fast, with consolidation already under way: Sitecore bought Scrunch, Adobe bought Semrush, and Ahrefs, Moz, SE Ranking and HubSpot all added AI-visibility modules. Several open-source trackers also exist. Entry prices run from about USD 25–30 (Local Falcon, Otterly) to USD 250–500 a month, and the products are built for brands and marketing teams measuring "share of voice" on English prompts. In Spanish/LatAm there are already at least six players: GEO Metrics (Spain, with Argentina as a priority market), SearchBrand.ai (Chile), Fardo (Argentina), ESBUENISIMO (Chile), plus agencies and free checkers, and in Brazil Promptado, Brasil GEO and First Answer. All of them sell brand-level visibility monitoring or consulting. **I found none that focuses on whether the facts are right (hours, prices, services) for small local businesses or professionals, or that fixes them.** Users complain that results are inconsistent, prices are high, and the tools say little about what to do.

### Cited Findings
**Prices (global tools; check before quoting, sources disagree)**
- Peec AI: Starter USD 95, Pro 245, Advanced 495 a month; extra models cost more; may have moved to euro pricing in some regions after 14 Aug 2026. Base plans cover ChatGPT, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Perplexity and Gemini (Meta AI not in base plans per Peec's own page). — [Frase](https://www.frase.io/blog/peec-ai-alternatives); [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/aeo-ai-visibility/best-peec-ai-alternatives); [Peec AI instructions page](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions)
- Profound: Starter USD 99/month (ChatGPT only, 50 prompts); USD 399 (three engines, 100 prompts, six articles); Enterprise custom, reportedly up to about USD 1,000/month for agencies. — [Digiday, 1 May 2026](https://digiday.com/marketing/marketers-question-expensive-ai-visibility-tools-as-inconsistent-results-fuel-skepticism/); [Pepper](https://www.pepper.inc/blog/alternatives-to-profound-8-modern-aeo-platforms-compared-2026/)
- Otterly.AI: from USD 29/month (15 prompts); tiers USD 189 and 489; Enterprise from USD 1,000. — [Ryze AI](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/ai-visibility-tools-pricing-compared-2026) (vendor-adjacent)
- Scrunch: Core USD 250/month; **acquired by Sitecore on 3 Jun 2026**. Covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Meta. — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/aeo-ai-visibility/best-peec-ai-alternatives); [Ryan Doser](https://ryandoser.com/profound-alternatives/)

## 4. Which of the proposed "new problems" are real, and which are already served?

### Takeaway
Real and under-served: (1) wrong facts about small businesses and professionals in AI answers; (2) stale or wrong local prices in an inflationary, dual-currency market; (3) the new "claim and manage your AI profile" layer (Businesses in ChatGPT, Nov 2026), which will need local operators. Real but hard to build on: monitoring Meta AI inside WhatsApp (no API; few trackers cover Meta AI, and none inside WhatsApp). Weak as a standalone business: llms.txt and "AI files" for SMBs, because the evidence says AI systems barely read them.

### Cited Findings
- **llms.txt does not move citations.** SE Ranking (about 300,000 domains, XGBoost model): having llms.txt did not make citation more likely, and only 10.13% of domains had one. Ahrefs (server logs of 137,210 domains): 97% of valid llms.txt files got no requests in the month studied. Google has not said it uses llms.txt for AI Overviews or AI Mode. — [Search Engine Journal (SE Ranking)](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/llms-txt-shows-no-clear-effect-on-ai-citations-based-on-300k-domains/561542/); [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/); [Search Engine Journal (Ahrefs)](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/97-of-llms-txt-files-got-no-requests-ahrefs-data-shows/)
- **Wrong facts are real and worse for SMBs**: Searchable (93% of London businesses described inaccurately; SMEs fabricated 5% vs 2%); SOCi (ChatGPT and Perplexity 68% accurate); Insites (56% and 48% full match). — sources in Q2.
- There is "no direct fix" for wrong business information in AI; the recommended cause and fix is inconsistent listings (Google Business Profile disagreeing with the website, etc.). — [Search Engine Journal: "AI answers about your locations are often wrong"](https://searchenginejournal.com/ai-answers-about-your-locations-are-often-wrong-check-before-customers-do/582205); [Localogy, Sep 2026](https://www.localogy.com/2026/09/ai-hallucinations-pose-risks-for-local-businesses/)

Businesses in ChatGPT now accepts product lists with prices and availability. — [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/chatgpt-launches-google-like-profiles-for-businesses)
- **Agentic commerce is arriving in Argentina**: Visa and Mastercard ran the first live agent-payment tests in Argentina in 2026; Mercado Libre / Mercado Pago is described as the most advanced case; Tiendanube integrated MODO. No formal Mercado Libre–OpenAI or Tiendanube–OpenAI deal was found. — [iProUP](https://www.iproup.com/finanzas/70397-mercado-libre-tiendanube-y-tarjetas-ya-prueban-compras-agenticas-en-argentina.amp). No tracker found queries Meta AI inside WhatsApp. — [Peec AI instructions](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions); [Brand24](https://brand24.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-tracking-tools/)
- **Professionals' reputation in AI**: Forbes Argentina tells professionals to audit "who appears instead of me" and "which topics AI associates me with" every six months. — [Forbes Argentina](https://www.forbesargentina.com/liderazgo/chatgpt-te-recomendaria-como-medir-visibilidad-tu-marca-personal-era-ia-n96054). ### Inferences
- "Machine-readable source of truth" is a real need, but the formats that matter are Google Business Profile, the website (with schema.org), Facebook, vertical directories, merchant feeds and now the ChatGPT business profile, not llms.txt. A product that only generates llms.txt files would sell a placebo.
- "Wrong prices or hours stated by AI" is real and documented (US/UK). In Argentina it is unmeasured but structurally worse (inflation, ARS/USD, Yelp missing).
- "Monitoring what Meta AI says in WhatsApp" is real demand in theory (WhatsApp is the main Meta AI surface and LatAm is WhatsApp-first), but there is no legitimate programmatic access inside WhatsApp. That makes a reliable product hard (automating WhatsApp accounts risks bans and breaks Meta's terms). A partial version can run on meta.ai (web), whose answers may differ from WhatsApp's.

### Gaps
- No measurement of how often AI answers give wrong prices for Argentine products or services.
- No evidence on whether Meta AI in WhatsApp cites or recommends local businesses in LatAm, or which sources it uses.
- Whether meta.ai (web) and Meta AI in WhatsApp answer the same way is unknown.

### Inferences

**G1. AI fact-check and correction for local SMBs, in LatAm Spanish ("¿Qué dice la IA de tu negocio, y es verdad?")**
- *Problem:* ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode, Perplexity and Meta AI state wrong hours, phone numbers, addresses, services or prices about small businesses, or leave them out. The owner never sees it, because the customer simply doesn't come.
- *Who suffers:* single-location businesses in Argentina and LatAm (clinics and dentists, gyms, workshops, real-estate agencies, restaurants, schools, dealers), and the local agencies that serve them.
- *Evidence:*
  - SMBs get at least one false fact 50% of the time vs 32% for large companies, and fabrication is 5% vs 2% (Searchable);
  - ChatGPT and Perplexity are about 68% accurate on profiles (SOCi), and only about 56% of ChatGPT answers fully match the Google profile (Insites);
  - ChatGPT recommends 1.2% of brand locations;
  - LatAm lacks Yelp, and Businesses in ChatGPT local claims are US-only, so the inputs are weaker;
  - Argentina has the highest weekly AI use (68%).
- *Product:* field-by-field verification (truth file vs what each AI says), prompts in local Spanish ("dentista en Nueva Córdoba que atienda OSDE"), run from the city, repeated over time to deal with variance, and the fix included: Google Business Profile, Facebook, schema.org on the website, directories. A fixed monthly price in ARS, or done-for-you. Channel: local agencies (white-label) and associations.
- *Differentiation vs GEO Metrics / SearchBrand.ai / Fardo:* those measure brand mention share for marketing teams. G1 measures **truth** and **fixes** it for SMBs. The "a score with no action" complaint is the opening.
- *Confidence nobody does this in LatAm:* **medium**. No Spanish SMB fact-check product was found, but GEO Metrics or SearchBrand.ai could add it, and Local Falcon or BrightLocal could localize.
- *Feasibility (3–6 months, solo):* **high**. APIs with web search (OpenAI, Gemini, Perplexity), plus forkable open-source trackers (Elmo, MIT), Google Business Profile APIs and Claude Code.
- *Durability:* **medium**. It is a commodity risk unless the moat is local data (a verified truth file per business, Argentine directory integrations, agency distribution) and a done-for-you service. It is helped by AI profiles becoming a permanent layer, as Google Business Profile did.
- *Revenue math (estimate, not sourced):* USD 1,000/month net ≈ 50 SMBs at about USD 20, or 10 agencies at about USD 100.

**G2. AI-readable price and stock "source of truth" for one vertical in an inflationary market (start: car dealers in Argentina)**
- *Problem:* when a buyer asks ChatGPT or AI Mode "¿cuánto sale el [modelo] 0 km en Córdoba?" or "¿qué concesionario tiene entrega inmediata?", the answer uses stale USD/ARS prices, old promos or national-level data, and rarely names the local dealer.
- *Who suffers:* dealers (and later real-estate agents, private clinics with self-pay price lists, schools, building suppliers).
- *Evidence:*
  - automotive answers carry citations about 20% of the time in ChatGPT, much more than other categories (Similarweb);
  - US dealers are already buying AI-visibility tools (DealerOn OnPrompt, CarGenius, Dealer.com GEO, BMW index); 84/100 US dealer sites "fail";
  - ChatGPT gets Argentine rates and prices wrong (iProfesional);
  - Businesses in ChatGPT accepts price and availability lists (US first);
  - agentic payments are being tested in Argentina (Visa/Mastercard 2026).
- *Product:* daily-updated public price, stock and promo pages per model and branch, with schema.org Vehicle/Offer markup and legal notes. Feeds formatted for Google Merchant Center / ChatGPT product lists where eligible. Weekly monitoring of what each AI says about "precio [modelo] [ciudad]", with alerts. A monthly "AI visibility report" by model.
- *Advantage:* he already produces content for a Córdoba dealer, a ready pilot and case study.
- *Confidence nobody does this in LatAm:* **medium-high**. No LatAm dealer AI-visibility product appeared in any search; the US players have no LatAm presence in the results.
- *Feasibility:* **high** technically. The hard part is getting price and stock data from each dealer (manual or spreadsheet at first).
- *Durability:* **medium-high** if it becomes data infrastructure (feeds) rather than a report; risk from portals (Mercado Libre, Autocosmos) and the OEMs' own sites.
- *Market size:* limited (a few hundred official dealers in Argentina), but expandable to other price-heavy verticals and countries.

**G3. AI reputation for licensed professionals (doctors, dentists, psychologists, lawyers, accountants, architects) in Spanish**
- *Problem:* patients and clients ask AI "¿quién es un buen [especialista] en [ciudad]?" or ask about a named professional. AI may leave them out, mix them up with someone who has the same name, show an old address or a wrong specialty or insurance, or link them to topics they don't want.
- *Evidence:*
  - professional services have the lowest citation presence (under 4%), so answers come from model memory, where fabrication happens;
  - 55% have used AI for health information (Doctoralia);
  - SMBs suffer more misattribution (4% vs 0.7%, Searchable);

**G4. Local operator for the new "AI profiles": Businesses in ChatGPT plus Google Business Profile, Bing and Apple listings, for LatAm SMBs**
- *Problem:* OpenAI launched claimable business profiles on 10 Nov 2026 (beta; local stores seemingly US-only; no multi-location management yet). When this reaches LatAm, millions of SMBs will need someone to claim, fill in (prices, products, hours), verify and keep profiles in sync across platforms, in Spanish.
- *Evidence:* the launch itself (NRN, VentureBeat, Rankability); Google Business Profile is the second-largest citation source for Google's AIs (BrightLocal); listings are about 42% of AI citations (Yext).
- *Competitors:* Yext, Uberall, Birdeye, SOCi and PinMeTo will integrate for enterprise and multi-location brands. No Spanish SMB-focused operator was found (not searched exhaustively).
- *Confidence:* **medium-low** that it stays unserved. It is a timing play; OpenAI may make self-serve easy, and Google Business Profile agencies will pivot.
- *Feasibility:* high as a service plus light software. *Durability:* depends on OpenAI's LatAm rollout (unknown date).
- *Best combined with G1:* the monitor finds the problems; profile management fixes them.

**G5 (weaker). Meta AI / WhatsApp answer monitoring for LatAm brands**

- *Confidence nobody does it inside WhatsApp:* medium. *Feasibility:* low-medium. *Durability:* low (one platform change kills it).
